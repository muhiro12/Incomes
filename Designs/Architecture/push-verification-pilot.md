# Push Verification Pilot (Historical)

The retired pilot compared private build, test, lint, and publication records
with the commits and destination of a proposed push. Its checker and dedicated
tests were removed when receipt matching was retired.

The [original pilot specification](https://github.com/muhiro12/Incomes/blob/c03329d2d5b598ddb04315ae4aaab17db0b401bd/Designs/Architecture/push-verification-pilot.md)
documents the historical format. For current checks and publication guidance,
see [Build and Test](../../README.md#build-and-test).
